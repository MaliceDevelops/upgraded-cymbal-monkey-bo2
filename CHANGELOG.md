# Changelog

## 1.0

First public release.

- Installs with my mod installer: Install.bat opens a window with a tick box for each of my mods, so you can add the [Apothicon Servant](https://github.com/MaliceDevelops/apothicon-servant-bo2) too. Only the ticked ones are downloaded, and they all work together in the same game, with or without Zombies Declassified: into Zombies Declassified, into a copy of it (your own stays as it is), or as a mod of their own. The installer stays on your PC with a "MaliceDevelops BO2 Mods" shortcut to add, remove, update or repair mods later.
